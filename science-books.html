SCIENCE BOOKS

The Science section of the CUSTECH University Library E-Library provides students, lecturers and researchers with access to electronic books and academic materials covering different areas of science and scientific studies.

The available resources cover subjects such as mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology and other science-related areas. The materials are intended to provide additional information that can support classroom learning, practical activities and academic research.

Students can use these resources to improve their understanding of scientific concepts, prepare for examinations, complete assignments and support their academic projects and research activities.
