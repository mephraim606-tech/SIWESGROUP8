CONTACT US / FEEDBACK

The CUSTECH University Library welcomes enquiries, suggestions, complaints, and feedback from students, staff, researchers, and other library users.

Your feedback helps the library understand the needs of its users and improve the quality of its resources, facilities, and services.

For general enquiries, you may contact the library through the official email address or visit the library during its opening hours.
