LIBRARY STAFF DIRECTORY

The CUSTECH University Library is supported by dedicated staff who assist students, lecturers, researchers, and other library users with access to information resources and library services.

Library staff perform different responsibilities including circulation services, cataloguing and classification, reference services, information literacy, e-library support, and general library administration.

Users can contact the appropriate staff member or visit the library for assistance with borrowing books, locating resources, accessing electronic materials, and other library-related enquiries.
