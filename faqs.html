FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

This section provides answers to common questions about the CUSTECH University Library, its resources, services, and the use of library facilities.

Students, staff, and other library users are encouraged to check these questions before contacting the library for assistance.

COMMON QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

1. Who can use the CUSTECH University Library?

The library is available to registered students, academic staff, non-academic staff, researchers, and other authorized users of the university.

2. How can I borrow a book from the library?

Users should locate the required book, present it at the circulation desk, and provide the necessary identification details for the borrowing process.

3. Can I renew a borrowed book?

Yes. Where renewal is permitted, users can request for the borrowing period to be extended before the book becomes overdue.

4. What can I do if the book I need is not available?

You can submit a book request through the library's Request a Book service. Provide the title, author, edition, and other relevant information about the material.

5. Does the library provide electronic resources?

Yes. The E-Library section provides access to electronic academic resources organized according to different faculties and subject areas.

6. Can I access past examination questions?

Yes. The Past Questions section provides a repository for examination papers and continuous assessment test papers where available.

7. Can I use the library for research?

Yes. The library supports academic research by providing access to books, electronic resources, past questions, reference materials, and other academic information resources.

8. What should I do if I cannot find a particular resource?

Ask a library staff member for assistance. Staff can help users locate available resources or provide guidance on alternative sources of information.

9. How can I contact the library?

You can visit the library or contact the library through library@custech.edu.ng for enquiries and assistance.

10. Where is the CUSTECH University Library located?

The library is located at Confluence University of Science and Technology, Osara, Kogi State.
