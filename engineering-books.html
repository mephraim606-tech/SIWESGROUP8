ENGINEERING BOOKS

The Engineering section of the CUSTECH University Library E-Library provides students, lecturers and researchers with access to electronic books and academic learning materials related to engineering and technological studies.

The resources in this section cover different areas of engineering, including electrical and electronic engineering, mechanical engineering, civil engineering, computer engineering, chemical engineering and other related technical subjects.

These materials are provided to support classroom learning, practical activities, assignments, examinations, projects and academic research.
