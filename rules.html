Library Rules and Regulations

The CUSTECH University Library is provided to support teaching, learning, research and other academic activities of the University. All library users are expected to follow the rules and regulations to maintain a peaceful, clean and productive environment.

Important Notice: All students, staff and other authorized library users are expected to observe the library rules. Failure to comply with the rules may result in appropriate action by the library authorities.

1. General Conduct

  1. Library users must maintain silence and avoid unnecessary conversations inside the reading areas.
  2. Mobile phones should be kept on silent mode while inside the library.
  3. Users must behave responsibly and respectfully towards other library users and members of staff.
  4. Running, shouting, fighting or any form of disruptive behaviour is not permitted within the library.
  5. Users must keep the library environment clean and properly dispose of waste in designated bins.

2. Use of Library Materials

  1. Library books and other materials must be handled carefully.
  2. Users must not tear, cut, fold or damage pages of library materials.
  3. Writing or making marks on library books and other materials is prohibited.
  4. Books taken from the shelves should be returned to the appropriate location or placed at the designated return area.
  5. Library materials must not be removed from the library without proper authorization or borrowing procedures.
  6. Users are responsible for materials borrowed under their names.

3. Borrowing and Returning Books

  1. Only registered and authorized library users may borrow library materials.
  2. A valid university identification card may be required when borrowing library materials.
  3. Borrowed materials must be returned on or before the assigned due date.
  4. Users should not lend borrowed library materials to another person.
  5. Overdue materials should be returned as soon as possible.
  6. Lost or damaged materials must be reported to the library authorities immediately.

4. Reading Room Regulations

  1. Seats in the reading room should not be reserved by leaving books, bags or other personal belongings unattended.
  2. Users should keep their study materials organized and avoid disturbing others.
  3. Sleeping in the reading area is discouraged, especially when seats are required by other users.
  4. Food and drinks should not be taken into areas where they may damage library materials or equipment.
  5. Users must leave their reading spaces clean after studying.

5. Use of Computers and Electronic Resources

  1. Library computers and electronic resources should be used primarily for academic and research purposes.
  2. Users must not intentionally damage or interfere with library computers, network equipment or other electronic facilities.
  3. Unauthorized installation of software on library computers is not permitted.
  4. Users should not use library computer systems for activities that may affect other users or library services.
  5. Users must log out of personal accounts after using a public computer.

6. Personal Belongings

  1. Users are responsible for their personal belongings while inside the library.
  2. Valuable items such as phones, laptops, wallets and other personal property should not be left unattended.
  3. Bags and other items should be kept only in areas designated by the library.
  4. The library shall not be responsible for loss or damage to unattended personal belongings.

7. Prohibited Activities

  1. Smoking is not permitted within the library premises.
  2. Consumption of alcohol or other prohibited substances is not allowed in the library.
  3. Fighting, harassment, bullying or threatening behaviour is prohibited.
  4. Defacing library property, furniture, books or equipment is prohibited.
  5. Unauthorized removal of library property is prohibited.
  6. Any activity that interferes with the peaceful use of the library is not permitted.

8. Compliance with Library Staff Instructions

Library users are expected to cooperate with library staff. Instructions given by authorized library personnel concerning the use of library facilities, materials and services should be followed.

Users who have questions or concerns about library rules should contact the library staff for clarification rather than disturbing other users.

9. Lost or Damaged Library Materials

Any library material that is lost or damaged while in the care of a user should be reported to the library as soon as possible. The library may require the user to replace the material or follow the applicable procedures for settlement of the loss or damage.

Warning: Users should not attempt to conceal lost or damaged library materials. Promptly reporting such incidents helps the library maintain accurate records and provide appropriate assistance.

10. Responsibility of Library Users

Every library user has a responsibility to protect library resources and contribute to a peaceful academic environment. Proper use of library facilities benefits students, staff, researchers and future users of the library.

Final Notice

The library reserves the right to take appropriate action when users fail to comply with established library rules and regulations. Library users are encouraged to seek assistance from library staff whenever they need help with library facilities, materials or services.

Remember:

Respect the library, protect library resources, maintain silence and allow other users to study and carry out their research without unnecessary disturbance.
