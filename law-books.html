LAW BOOKS

The Law section of the CUSTECH University Library E-Library provides students, lecturers and researchers with access to electronic books and academic learning materials related to legal studies and the practice of law.

The collection covers areas such as constitutional law, criminal law, civil law, contract law, commercial law, company law, property law, legal research and other related areas of legal studies.

These resources support classroom learning, assignments, examinations, legal research, academic projects and other learning activities. Students can use the materials to improve their understanding of legal principles, concepts and procedures.
