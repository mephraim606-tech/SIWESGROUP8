Meet the University Librarian

The University Librarian is responsible for providing professional leadership and direction for the activities and development of the CUSTECH University Library.

The office works to ensure that library resources and services effectively support teaching, learning, research and the academic development of members of the university community.

Mrs. Helen Chris-Israel

Second Substantive University Librarian

Mrs. Helen Chris-Israel is the Second Substantive University Librarian of the Confluence University of Science and Technology (CUSTECH), Osara.

She brings over two decades of professional experience in librarianship, research and information management across academic, media and institutional library environments.

Her professional interests and experience include knowledge organization, academic research services, information literacy and institutional library development.

Academic Qualifications

The University Librarian has professional academic qualifications in Library and Information Science.

  • Bachelor of Library and Information Science (BLIS) – Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria.
  • Master of Library and Information Science (MLIS) – Adeleke University, Ede.

Professional Experience

Mrs. Chris-Israel has over 20 years of professional experience in the library and information science field.

Her experience covers different areas of library practice, including academic library services, research support, information management and modern library administration.

Her experience across different library environments contributes to the effective management and development of library services at CUSTECH.

Professional Experience

Over two decades of experience in librarianship, research and information management.

Areas of Expertise

The professional expertise of the University Librarian covers important areas of modern librarianship and academic information services.

  • Knowledge organization
  • Academic research services
  • Information literacy
  • Information management
  • Institutional library development
  • Modern library administration
  • Research and academic support

Role of the University Librarian

The Office of the University Librarian provides leadership for the effective operation and development of the University Library.

Some important areas of responsibility include:

  • Providing professional leadership for library operations and services.
  • Supporting the provision and organization of academic information resources.
  • Promoting effective library services for students, lecturers and researchers.
  • Supporting information literacy and effective use of information resources.
  • Encouraging the development of modern library and information services.
  • Supporting teaching, learning and research activities within the university.
  • Promoting professional development and improvement of library services.

Professional Memberships

The University Librarian is professionally connected with organizations and bodies associated with librarianship and information services.

  • Nigerian Library Association (NLA)
  • Librarians' Registration Council
  • Other professional library and information science associations

Leadership and Library Development

Effective library leadership is important to the growth of an academic institution. The Office of the University Librarian works toward improving access to information resources and strengthening services that meet the changing needs of students and researchers.

Through professional knowledge, research support and modern information services, the University Library continues to serve as an important part of the academic environment of CUSTECH.

Message to Library Users

Students and members of the university community are encouraged to make effective use of the library and its available information resources.

Users should develop good reading and research habits, respect library facilities and observe the rules and regulations provided for the proper use of the library.

The Library is Here to Support You

Make the library part of your academic journey by reading, researching and making responsible use of available information resources.
