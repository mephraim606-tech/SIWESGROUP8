PAST QUESTIONS

The CUSTECH University Library provides a repository of past questions and test papers to support students in their academic preparation.

Past questions can help students understand the type of questions that may be asked in their courses, identify important topics and improve their preparation for examinations and continuous assessment tests.

Students can use this section to access examination papers and C.A. test papers according to the available categories in the library repository.

Important:

Past questions are provided as academic study and revision resources. Students should combine them with lecture notes, textbooks, practical exercises and other recommended learning materials.

HOW TO USE THE PAST QUESTIONS REPOSITORY

  1. Select the type of past question you need, such as Examination Papers or C.A. Test Papers.
  2. Browse the available course or subject categories.
  3. Select the relevant course or examination material.
  4. Review the questions carefully and use them for revision and practice.
  5. Compare your answers with your lecture notes, textbooks and other approved academic materials.

BENEFITS OF USING PAST QUESTIONS

01

Understand Question Patterns

Reviewing previous questions can help students become familiar with different ways academic topics may be assessed.

02

Improve Revision

Past questions can help students identify areas that require additional study and revision.

03

Practise Before Examinations

Students can use previous questions to practise answering questions before an examination or test.

ACADEMIC CATEGORIES

The availability of materials may differ between courses and academic sessions. Students should check the relevant repository section for the materials currently available.

TIPS FOR EFFECTIVE USE

  • Start your revision early instead of waiting until the examination period.
  • Attempt questions yourself before checking your notes or other materials.
  • Pay attention to topics that appear repeatedly across different question papers.
  • Use a timer when practising examination questions to improve your time management.
  • Discuss difficult questions with your lecturers, classmates or study group where appropriate.
  • Do not rely exclusively on past questions. Study the full course content as well.
Academic Use Notice

Past questions are intended to support academic revision and preparation. The appearance of a question in a previous paper does not guarantee that the same question will appear in a future examination or test.

Students should study their current course materials and follow instructions provided by their lecturers.
