EXAMINATION PAST QUESTIONS REPOSITORY

Welcome to the CUSTECH University Library Examination Past Questions Repository.

This section provides students with access to previous examination papers for academic revision, preparation and practice.

Students can browse the available examination materials according to their academic area and select the relevant course or subject.

Study Reminder:

Past examination questions should be used together with lecture notes, textbooks, practical exercises and other approved learning materials.

HOW TO ACCESS EXAMINATION PAPERS

  1. Identify your faculty or academic area.
  2. Select the relevant department or subject.
  3. Select the course code or course title.
  4. Select the available academic session or semester where applicable.
  5. Open or download the examination paper.
  6. Use the questions for revision and practice.

TIPS FOR USING EXAMINATION PAST QUESTIONS

  • Attempt the questions yourself before checking your lecture notes.
  • Use a timer to practise answering questions within an examination period.
  • Identify topics that require additional study.
  • Compare your answers with relevant course materials.
  • Discuss difficult questions with your lecturer or study group.
  • Do not depend entirely on past questions when preparing for examinations.
Important Academic Notice

Past examination questions are provided for academic revision and practice. The appearance of a question in a previous examination does not guarantee that the same question will be repeated.

Students are encouraged to study the complete course content and follow the instructions provided by their lecturers.
