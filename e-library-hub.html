E-LIBRARY

The CUSTECH University Library E-Library provides students, lecturers, researchers and other members of the university community with access to electronic academic resources.

The E-Library is designed to support teaching, learning, research and independent study by providing useful educational materials in digital form.

Electronic books and other academic resources are arranged according to different faculties and areas of study. Users can select their field of study below to explore available resources and find materials relevant to their courses and academic activities.

Students are encouraged to make proper use of the E-Library as an additional source of learning materials alongside the physical resources available in the university library.
