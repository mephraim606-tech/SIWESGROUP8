Opening and Closing Hours

The CUSTECH University Library provides access to its facilities and resources during the hours shown below. Students and other authorized users are encouraged to plan their study and research activities within the available library hours.

Library Hours:

Users should arrive early enough to complete their activities before the library closes.

Study and Research Hours

Students are encouraged to make effective use of the library during the available opening hours for reading, studying, research, accessing library materials and using available electronic resources.

Before the Library Closes

  1. Complete your borrowing, returning or other library transactions before closing time.
  2. Return reading materials to the appropriate place or designated return area.
  3. Save your work and properly log out when using library computers.
  4. Collect all personal belongings before leaving the library.
  5. Leave your study area clean and organized.

Special Opening Hours

Library opening hours may be adjusted during examinations, public holidays, university events, semester breaks or other special academic periods.

Users are advised to check library announcements or contact library staff whenever they need information about changes to the normal schedule.

Important:

The opening hours displayed on this page represent the regular schedule. Special circumstances may result in temporary changes to the library operating hours.
