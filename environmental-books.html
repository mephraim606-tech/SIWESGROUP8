ENVIRONMENTAL SCIENCES BOOKS

The Environmental Sciences section of the CUSTECH University Library E-Library provides students, lecturers and researchers with access to electronic books and academic learning materials related to environmental studies and the built environment.

The collection covers areas such as environmental science, environmental management, geography, urban and regional planning, surveying, quantity surveying, building technology and other related environmental and built-environment subjects.

These resources support classroom learning, practical activities, assignments, examinations, academic projects and research. Students can use the materials to improve their understanding of environmental concepts and develop knowledge that can help address environmental and development challenges.
