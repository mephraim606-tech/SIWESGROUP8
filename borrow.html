Book Borrowing Policy

Before applying to borrow a book from the CUSTECH University Library, students are required to read and understand the borrowing policies below.

Important Notice

Borrowed library materials remain the responsibility of the student until they are properly returned to the library.

1. Borrowing Eligibility

  1. Only registered students and other authorized users of the University Library may borrow library materials.
  2. Students must provide valid identification or other required details when applying to borrow a book.
  3. Borrowing privileges may be subject to the library's borrowing requirements and available resources.

2. Borrowing Rules

  1. Students must complete the borrowing application correctly before a book can be issued.
  2. Books may only be borrowed through the approved library borrowing procedure.
  3. Students must provide accurate information on the borrowing application form.
  4. Borrowed books must not be transferred or lent to another person.
  5. Students are responsible for all books borrowed using their details.

3. Care of Borrowed Books

  1. Borrowed books must be kept in good condition.
  2. Students must not tear, cut, remove or damage pages.
  3. Writing, drawing or marking inside library books is not permitted.
  4. Books should be protected from water, food, fire and other conditions that may cause damage.

4. Returning Borrowed Books

  1. Books must be returned on or before the assigned return date.
  2. Students should ensure that returned books are properly recorded by the library.
  3. Students should not leave borrowed books unattended without following the library's return procedure.

5. Lost or Damaged Books

A student who loses or damages a borrowed library book must report the situation to the library as soon as possible.

The library may require the student to follow the appropriate replacement or settlement procedure.

Warning:

Students should not conceal lost or damaged books. Report the problem to the library as soon as possible so that the appropriate procedure can be followed.

6. Renewal of Borrowed Books

Where renewal is permitted, students may request a renewal before the book's due date, subject to the library's renewal conditions and availability of the material.

A book may not be renewable where another library user has requested or reserved the same material.

7. Student Responsibility

  1. Provide correct information during the borrowing application.
  2. Keep borrowed materials safe.
  3. Return borrowed materials according to the required procedure.
  4. Report lost or damaged materials promptly.
  5. Follow instructions given by authorized library staff.

Borrowing Agreement

By proceeding to the borrowing application, I confirm that I have read and understood the library borrowing policy and agree to follow the rules guiding the use and borrowing of library materials.
