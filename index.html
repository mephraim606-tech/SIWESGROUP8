Welcome Message

Welcome to the CUSTECH University Library Portal. The library provides students and staff with access to academic materials that support their studies, research and personal development.

Through this portal, users can learn about library services, check book categories, access past examination questions, submit book requests and find important information about library rules and opening hours.
